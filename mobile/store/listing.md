## Full description  (max 4000 characters)

Al‑Muqri (المقرئ) helps you check and improve your Qur'an recitation, privately, right on your phone.

Select any word, verse, or passage, recite it, and Al‑Muqri instantly tells you which words were correct (green) and which need work (red) — highlighting mistakes in vowels (harakāt), elongation (madd), and letter articulation. Everything runs on your device, so it works with no internet and none of your voice ever leaves your phone.

■ KEY FEATURES

• On‑device recitation checking — recite a word or phrase and see instant green/red feedback, powered by an AI phonetic model that runs entirely on your phone.
• Works offline — the recitation engine and the full Qur'an text (all 114 surahs) are on your device. No account, no sign‑in.
• Live mode — press Start and recite freely; words light up as you go, anywhere on the page.
• Word‑by‑word listening — hear each selected word recited clearly (mujawwad).
• Tajweed listening — hear the flowing recitation of Sheikh Mahmoud Khalil Al‑Husary for a selected passage.
• Mistake breakdown — a panel lists exactly which letters, vowels, or madд lengths were off, in Arabic and English.
• Clean mushaf reader — adjustable text size, page navigation, and a distraction‑free layout.
• Private by design — your microphone audio is analyzed on the device and is never uploaded or stored on any server.

■ HOW IT WORKS

1. Pick a surah and page.
2. Tap or drag to select the word(s) you want to practice.
3. Recite — Al‑Muqri scores each word and points out any mistakes.
4. Tap "Listen" to hear the correct recitation, then try again.

■ ABOUT THE ENGINE

Al‑Muqri's feedback is powered by the open recitation model "quran‑muaalem" (Wav2Vec2‑BERT multi‑level CTC), which analyzes recitation at the level of the Qur'anic Phonetic Script — vowels, madd length, and articulation attributes.

Some reference audio for a chapter is downloaded the first time you use it and then cached for offline use.
